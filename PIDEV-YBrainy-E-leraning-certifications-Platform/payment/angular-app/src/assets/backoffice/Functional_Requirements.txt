
# Functional Requirements Specification (Cahier des Charges Fonctionnel)

## 1. Administrator Functional Requirements
**Objective:** Provide full control over the platform's users, content, finances, and security.

*   **User Management:**
    *   Manage users (students, teachers).
    *   Assign, update, and revoke user roles and permissions.
    *   Activate, suspend, or delete user accounts.
    *   View user login history and activity logs.

*   **Content & Certification Management:**
    *   Manage courses, services, and certifications.
    *   Approve, edit, or remove courses created by teachers.
    *   Manage certification sources and dropshipping providers.
    *   Monitor trending certifications and learning demands.
    *   Create and manage learning packs and subscription plans.

*   **Analytics & Monitoring:**
    *   Supervise overall platform activity.
    *   Monitor student performance and engagement statistics.
    *   Access AI-based analytics and predictive dashboards.

*   **Security & Finance:**
    *   Manage access control and security policies.
    *   Monitor fraud detection alerts and suspicious payments.
    *   Validate and refund transactions when necessary.
    *   View financial dashboards, revenues, and transaction history.

*   **Communication & Community:**
    *   Manage automated emails, notifications, and announcements.
    *   Moderate forums and reported content.

---

## 2. Student Functional Requirements
**Objective:** Provide a personalized and engaging learning experience for end-users.

*   **Authentication & Profile:**
    *   Register and log in securely.
    *   Use optional AI-based face recognition for authentication.
    *   Manage personal profile and preferences.

*   **Learning & Enrollment:**
    *   Access online courses, video courses, and live sessions.
    *   Enroll in courses, learning packs, and certifications.
    *   Take quizzes, exams, and certification assessments.

*   **Intelligence & Recommendations:**
    *   Receive AI-personalized content and course recommendations.
    *   Get suggested certifications based on performance and CV analysis.

*   **Progress & Records:**
    *   Track learning progress, scores, and completion status.
    *   Download course completion certificates.

*   **Commerce & Communication:**
    *   Purchase subscriptions and certification packs.
    *   Receive automated emails and notifications.
    *   Participate in forums and discussions.
    *   Communicate with teachers through messaging tools.
    *   View payment history and invoices.

---

## 3. Teacher Functional Requirements
**Objective:** Empower instructors to create content, assess students, and manage their classes.

*   **Content Creation:**
    *   Create, update, and manage courses (video & live).
    *   Upload course materials (videos, documents, quizzes).
    *   Define learning levels (beginner, intermediate, advanced).
    *   Create quizzes, exams, and certification preparation content.

*   **Assessment & Monitoring:**
    *   Configure adaptive quizzes based on student level.
    *   Monitor student progress, performance, and engagement.
    *   Access AI-based analytics on student results.

*   **Engagement & Feedback:**
    *   Recommend courses and certifications to students.
    *   Interact with students via forums, messaging, and announcements.
    *   Moderate course-related discussions.
    *   Schedule live classes and manage attendance.

*   **Performance Tracking:**
    *   Receive feedback and ratings from students.
    *   Track course popularity and completion rates.
