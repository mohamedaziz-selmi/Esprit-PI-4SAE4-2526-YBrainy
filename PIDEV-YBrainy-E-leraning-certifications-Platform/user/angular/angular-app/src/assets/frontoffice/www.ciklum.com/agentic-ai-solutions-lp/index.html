FAQs on Agentic AI Services

Ciklum delivers fully functional Agentic AI Services in just 5 weeks, starting with a production-ready proof of concept (POC). Our approach minimizes internal strain by integrating with your existing systems (like CRM or ERP) via APIs, requiring only essential access to relevant data and workflows. The result? Rapid deployment with minimal disruption – so your AI agents are up and running fast.

Unlike traditional RPA or rule-based automation, Ciklum’s Agentic AI Solution brings autonomous reasoning into your workflows. Our AI agents don’t just follow rules – they act with context and confidence, drawing from past interactions, unstructured data, and internal logic to resolve tickets, write SQL, or fix data pipeline issues without human handoffs. It’s Agentic Process Automation (APA) that adapts, not just executes.

Ciklum’s Agentic AI Solution is designed with enterprise-grade security in mind. We follow best practices for encryption, role-based access control (RBAC), and data anonymization to protect sensitive business and customer information. Our AI agents operate within secure, governed environments to meet compliance standards across BFSI, healthcare, and other regulated industries.

We track success where it matters – adoption, efficiency, and business impact. KPIs include task resolution time (e.g., reducing workflows from days to minutes), reduction in human workload, CSAT improvements, and NPS. Our AI agents are monitored in production, tested with real users, and iterated to maximize ROI – not just launched and left.

To scope your Agentic AI Solution accurately, we’ll need a clear view of the business processes you want to automate, existing tools and systems (e.g., Salesforce, Snowflake), and any datasets the AI agents need to learn from or interact with. From there, we can quickly define the POC, architecture, integration points, and deliver a tailored proposal that fits your goals.
