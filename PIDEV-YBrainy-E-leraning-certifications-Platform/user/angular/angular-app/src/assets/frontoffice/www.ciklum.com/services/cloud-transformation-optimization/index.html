    Discovery & Assessment

    Identify assets, map dependencies, and establish performance and cost baselines.

    Design & Planning

    Design the target cloud-native or hybrid architecture and create a detailed, wave-based migration plan.

    Optimization & Refactoring

    Perform targeted code optimization and refactor bottlenecks *before* the move for day-one performance.

    Migration & Execution

    Use Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and automation to transfer applications and data with minimal downtime.

    Validation & Tuning

    Run comprehensive tests and tune configurations to validate functionality and FinOps goals.

    Handover & Continuous Optimization

    Provide full documentation, train your teams, and establish ongoing governance.

    More Than Just Migration

    As an expanded service, our approach breaks down the traditional borders between application development and infrastructure engineering. We bring an engineering-first mindset to cloud transformation.

    Wider Technological Flexibility

    We leverage deep expertise in Golang (Go) and Python to refactor bottlenecks at the code level, not just the infrastructure level.

    A Holistic, Engineering-First View

    We see your platform as a single product, ensuring infrastructure decisions serve the application and the application is optimized for the cloud.

    A Proven, Methodical Process

    We bring new tools, a rigorous methodology, and a deep library of proven case studies to de-risk your transformation and accelerate value.
