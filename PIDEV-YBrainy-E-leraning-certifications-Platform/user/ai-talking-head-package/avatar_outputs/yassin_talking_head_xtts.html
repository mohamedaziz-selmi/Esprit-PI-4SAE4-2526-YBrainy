Talking Head XTTS Demo

Type a line, press speak, and the popup head will float with smoother lip-sync and occasional blinking. Later on your site you can call window.talkingHead.say(...) directly.

This demo sends text to the local XTTS clone backend, plays the generated wav, and drives the mouth from the returned audio energy. Fresh lines can take a while to generate.
